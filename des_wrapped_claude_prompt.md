
# DES WRAPPED: 28
Build a mobile-first interactive cinematic birthday web experience for my girlfriend Des.

This is NOT a slideshow.
This is NOT a scrapbook.
This should feel like:
- Spotify Wrapped
- Apple keynote
- A24 emotional storytelling
- premium editorial design
- playful relationship artifact

TECH STACK
- Frontend-only MVP
- Next.js + Tailwind + Framer Motion
- Mobile-first ONLY
- Fullscreen vertical sections/cards
- Smooth cinematic transitions
- Dark/light cinematic aesthetic
- No backend required
- Local image assets only
- Prioritize polish over architecture

OVERALL EXPERIENCE
The app tells the story of Des turning 29 and recaps her 28th year.
Tone should alternate between:
- emotional
- funny
- cinematic
- chaotic
- romantic
- stylish

The experience eventually transitions into a cat phone case selection game.
At the end, reveal that she is getting a new iPhone 17.
